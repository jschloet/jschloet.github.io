Challenge the AI

How does experience change an AI's strategy? Play the coin-placement game against real neural networks trained for different numbers of self-play games.

The challenge

Players alternate placing identical coins inside the circle, without overlapping. The player who cannot place a coin loses. You play against a trained AI. Change its checkpoint to compare how its decisions evolved during training. Unlike the original coin game, this experiment uses fixed grid positions for the coin centres.

What should you look for?

Does the AI take the centre? Does it divide the board into regions? Can you beat an earlier checkpoint and then try the same idea against a later one?

About this experiment: All five checkpoints were trained on a 15 × 15 grid, with coin radius 1.7 and board radius 7 in grid units. Legal coin centres are restricted to grid points where the entire coin fits. This version performs neural-network inference entirely in your browser; there is no server-side game engine and no training during play. Model files are downloaded from the same static site when selected. The learned policy's behavior may differ from the continuous board used in the earlier activity.
